how to connect ghl and yelp

GoHighLevel (GHL) does not have a single 1-click native toggle for Yelp, so how you connect them depends on whether you want to capture inbound Yelp leads, send review requests, or display Yelp reviews on funnels/dashboards.

1. Inbound Leads (Yelp Messages & Quotes → GHL)

Yelp does not allow public webhooks directly to CRM systems without Yelp Partner API access, but you can capture leads via:

Email Parsing (Easiest & Free):

Yelp sends an email notification every time a lead requests a quote or sends a message.

Forward these emails into a parsing tool (e.g., Zapier Email Parser, Make.com mailhook, or GHL Inbound Webhook via an email router like SendGrid/Mailgun).

Extract the lead’s name, phone number, email, and inquiry message.

Pass that data into GHL using the Create/Update Contact action, apply a tag like source: yelp, and add them to an Opportunity pipeline.

Third-Party Marketplace Connectors:

Specialized GHL Marketplace apps or webhook bridge services (like lead-routing middleware) connect directly to Yelp’s Messaging API to sync replies two-way into GHL Conversations.

2. Reputation Management (Sending Review Requests)

To route customers to leave a review on Yelp via GHL review workflows:

Grab your Yelp Direct Review Link:

Go to your Yelp business listing.

Click Write a Review, then copy the full URL from the browser bar (it typically ends in /write_review).

Add to GHL Settings:

In your Sub-Account, go to Settings → Reputation Management.

Under Review Link, paste your direct Yelp link into your custom review URL field (or use it inside custom values).

Trigger via Workflow:

Build an automation (Automation → Workflows).

Trigger on appointment completion, invoice payment, or tag addition.

Send an SMS or Email containing the link: Hey {{contact.first_name}}, we'd love your feedback! Please leave us a review here: [Yelp Custom Link].

3. Displaying Yelp Reviews on Funnels & Websites

Yelp strictly protects its data, but you can embed reviews directly onto your GHL funnels:

Native Yelp Embed (Single Reviews):

Find a specific review on your Yelp page.  
WiserNotify

Click the three dots/share icon and select Embed review.

In the GHL Website/Funnel builder, add a Custom JS/HTML element and paste the snippet.  
WiserNotify

Aggregator Widgets (Live Carousel / Grid):

Use a widget provider like Elfsight, SociableKIT, or WiserNotify.  
SociableKIT

Connect your Yelp Business URL to the aggregator to generate a responsive embed script.

Paste that snippet into an HTML block or custom code section in GHL.

Option 1: Mobile App Push Notifications → Webhook → Zapier → GHL

(Best if staff manages the Yelp Inbox via a desktop web browser)

Option 3: Yelp Fusion Partner API / Lead Middleware

(Best for agencies or multi-location setups)

Standard free Yelp Fusion API keys only allow read access to public reviews and business details—not private user leads or messages. However:

Official Yelp Partner Access: If the business spends on Yelp Ads or applies for Partner API access, Yelp provides webhook endpoints for the Messaging / Request-a-Quote API.

Pre-Built Lead Middleware: Services like LeadBridge, LeadsBridge, or ZapERP maintain official Yelp partner status. You authenticate your Yelp Business login through their platform, and they provide a direct integration or outbound webhook to Zapier/GHL.

Option 4: Ask for an Email Auto-Forwarding Rule (Zero Email Access Needed)

If someone else owns the email inbox (the business owner or IT admin), you don't need ongoing access to their inbox:

Zapier Email Parser uses positional matching and surrounding anchor text, not semantic/contextual AI understanding.

How it operates:

Bounding Anchors: When you highlight Every week, the parser looks for the static text immediately before it (How often do you want your home cleaned?\n) and immediately after it (\n\nDo you need any of these other services?).

Line Position & Flow: It expects the text to appear in roughly the same relative structural order.

It does not read meaning: If a future Yelp email phrases the question differently (e.g., "Frequency:" instead of "How often do you want your home cleaned?"), or skips an optional question entirely, positional parser engines can break or pull empty data.

Why "Extra Templates" Matter:

Saving multiple Extra Templates for variations (one with notes, one without notes, one for move-out cleans vs deep cleans) trains Zapier's engine to recognize the different positional layouts.

No. It is technically impossible to extract a customer’s real, direct email address (e.g., @gmail.com, @icloud.com) from Yelp—whether through scraping tools, GitHub repositories, or official APIs.

Yelp Does Not Store or Render Customer Emails in the UI:

When a user requests a quote or sends a message, their personal email address is never displayed on the webpage or transmitted in backend API responses. Even if you inspect network traffic inside the Yelp for Business Leads Center, Yelp only references the user by their internal customer token or conversation ID (reply+...-@messaging.yelp.com). There is no email string in the DOM for a scraper to extract.

Yelp’s Business Model Depends on Walled Messaging:

Yelp intentionally shields consumer contact details behind their proxy relay and mobile app to:

Protect user privacy and comply with data privacy regulations (CCPA/GDPR).
