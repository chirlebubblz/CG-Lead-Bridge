### [1:15 – 2:05] Inside the GHL Build: Custom Fields, Pipeline & Smart Workflows
*(Screen Share — Show GHL Workflow Builder: If/Else conditions, Delays, and Custom Fields)*

> "Here is how I structured that inside GoHighLevel:
>
> • **Standardized Custom Fields:** I organized 7 dedicated fields in a `Yelp Lead Details` folder—capturing bedrooms, bathrooms, service type, and frequency directly from the survey.  
> • **Dedicated 8-Stage Pipeline:** Deal cards are created instantly under `New Leads` and advance through stages like `Quoted`, `Follow-up Automated`, and `Closed Won`.  
> • **The Workflow Automation:**  
> &nbsp;&nbsp;1. **A Data Guardrail:** An If/Else branch checks if bed and bath counts exist. If missing, it automatically asks the homeowner for their property specifications.  
> &nbsp;&nbsp;2. **The 90-Second Smart Buffer:** A conditional wait step that gives the system time to merge secondary cell phone submissions before internal alerts fire.  
> &nbsp;&nbsp;3. **2-Way Chat Bubble Ingestion:** Using Yelp's email relay and GHL's Inbound Conversations API, customer messages appear directly as **live chat bubbles inside GHL Conversations**. Reps reply directly from GHL, and it renders back to the homeowner’s Yelp app in seconds—protecting the business's *'Replies in minutes'* badge."
