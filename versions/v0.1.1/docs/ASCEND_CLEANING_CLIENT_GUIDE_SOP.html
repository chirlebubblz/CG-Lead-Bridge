1. Addressing Client Concerns Directly Non-Negotiable Architecture
Lead Nurturing

Concern: "Automations built out for nurturing leads with automatic messages"

Resolved: Deployed 4 dedicated automated sequences: 2 for Yelp leads (24h/72h quote follow-ups + 48h/Day 5 revival), 1 for Meta/Web leads (30-day 6-touch sequence), and 1 for past clients (14-day/21-day recurring upsell).

Safety Shield

Concern: "Make sure it doesn’t blast people that had a bad experience or asked for refunds"

Resolved: Built a 3-layer protection shield. Past clients only enter if tagged one-time-good. All workflows evaluate pre-send guardrails, and Workflow C acts as an instant circuit breaker upon tagging bad-experience or refund-requested.

Zero Discounts

Concern: "Let's not do promos, client did not allow promos"

Resolved: Completely removed all percentage-off and dollar-off incentives. All follow-up copy centers on priority weekend scheduling, custom cleaning checklists, and dedicated cleaner consistency.

Yelp Chat Bubble

Concern: "Yelp messages must land where the customer actually looks"

Resolved: Outbound messages to Yelp leads are dispatched via Yelp's Relay Email (leadsapi+...). Yelp instantly translates these into push notifications and native in-app chat bubbles with zero carrier SMS filtering risk.

4. Daily Staff Operating Procedures (SOP) Team Action Guide
1. New Yelp Lead Intake
  1. Lead automatically lands in GHL Conversations & Pipeline 5 (New Leads).
  2. Review questionnaire (Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Notes) in custom fields.
  3. Send customer greeting and initial estimate inside the chat thread.
2. When a Quote is Sent
  1. Drag opportunity card from New Leads to Quoted.
  2. This triggers the 24h & 72h automated Yelp follow-up sequence.
  3. Sequence automatically halts as soon as customer replies.
3. Safety Disarm: Unhappy / Refund
  1. If customer complains or requests a refund, open their contact card.
  2. Apply tag: bad-experience OR refund-requested.
  3. Circuit breaker activates: Contact is instantly ejected from all marketing, SMS is suppressed, and management is alerted.
4. Post-Cleaning Routine
  1. Following a clean, confirm customer satisfaction.
  2. If clean was a success, apply tag: one-time-good.
  3. In 14 days, the system follows up to lock in their recurring schedule.
  4. If unverified, use one-time-unknown to keep them safe from automated messages.
