3 Smart Auto-Responder & 2-Way Live Chat
⚡ The 90-Second Smart Auto-Responder

After a lead arrives, the system waits 90 seconds to allow Yelp to pass the customer's phone number. It then routes automatically:

  • Phone provided: Instant SMS greeting asking for square footage and preferred days.
  • No phone (Yelp relay only): Instant response sent directly into the customer's Yelp chat — no phone required.
💬 True 2-Way Live Chat — No Yelp Login Needed

Your staff never needs the Yelp Business app or shared login passwords:

  • Yelp customer messages arrive as Live Chat bubbles in GHL Conversations.
  • Replies typed in GHL are delivered back to the customer's Yelp app within 5 seconds.
  • Complete conversation history stays in one place — GHL.
💡
How to Reply to a Yelp Lead Inside GHL — Step by Step:
  1. Open GoHighLevel → Conversations and find the lead's card (it will appear as a Live Chat message).
  2. In the message composer at the bottom of the thread, click the channel dropdown and select Email — this routes your reply through Yelp's secure masked relay address.
  3. Do NOT enter a Subject line. Leave it blank. Simply type your message in the body field.
  4. Hit Send. Your message is automatically delivered into the customer's Yelp chat interface within seconds.
⚠️
Testing Guardrail: When testing, always close biz.yelp.com and the Yelp for Business mobile app first. Yelp detects active sessions and suppresses email notifications — which stops Zapier from triggering and the bridge from receiving anything.
