# Yelp ↔ GoHighLevel Integration & Troubleshooting Runbook

This document serves as the comprehensive technical guide for managing, maintaining, and troubleshooting the Yelp to GoHighLevel (Clean Genie) lead ingestion and 2-way conversation pipeline.

---

## 1. Core Architecture & Email Routing

Yelp generates two distinct email formats for any consumer inquiry. Understanding the difference is vital for maintaining conversation continuity and preventing duplicate CRM records:

| Email Format | Generated By | Purpose | In GHL Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`leadsapi+<hex>@messaging.yelp.com`** | **Yelp Lead Generation Engine** | Created the millisecond a consumer submits a "Request a Quote" form. Carries initial metadata, survey questions, and project category. | **Primary Contact Email.** Supports full 2-way messaging directly from GHL Conversations. |
| **`reply+<hex>@messaging.yelp.com`** | **Yelp Consumer Messaging Relay** | Generated dynamically by Yelp's notification mail server for consumer privacy. Appears in the `From:` / `Reply-To:` headers of email alerts. | **Secondary / Redundant.** Routes to the same Yelp thread, but should NOT be used as a separate contact in GHL. |

> **Key Rule:** Yelp's mail servers accept replies sent to **`leadsapi+...`** and post them straight into the customer's Yelp in-app chat. Both addresses resolve to the same underlying conversation thread on Yelp.

## 2. Preventing Duplicate Contacts in GoHighLevel

### The Cause of Duplicates
Running two simultaneous automations:
1. **Zap 1 (`Yelp Leads to Go High Level Contact`):** Ingests the lead immediately via Yelp's API using `leadsapi+...`.
2. **Zap 2 (`Yelp Email to GHL Contact Detail`):** Parses incoming Gmail notification emails with AI and creates a second contact using `reply+...`.

Because GoHighLevel deduplicates contacts based on matching Email and Phone, differing email strings cause GHL to create two separate contacts for the exact same person.

### The Production Standard
* **Run ONLY Zap 1 (`Yelp Leads to Go High Level Contact`):**
  * Establishes `leadsapi+...` as the primary identifier.
  * Ingests Name, Service Type, and Initial Survey Details.
  * Preserves single-contact integrity with zero duplicate record generation.
* **If Zap 2 is ever utilized:** It must **never** create contacts. It must execute a **Find Contact** action (by name/lead ID) followed by an **Update Contact** action targeting custom fields only.

## 3. Capturing Customer Phone Numbers (`Phone Availability`)

### Why `New Lead` Has an Empty Phone Field
1. In Yelp's consumer flow, the user submits their project questions (Step 1: Lead Created).
2. The `New Lead` trigger fires **instantly** at that millisecond.
3. Yelp then renders a secondary, post-submission confirmation screen asking the consumer if they would like to share their direct phone number.
4. If the consumer enters a phone number (e.g. `(312) 505-6761`), Yelp updates the lead record 1–5 seconds later. Because `New Lead` has already executed, its static snapshot contains an empty phone field.

### The Solution: Companion "Phone Availability" Zap
To ensure 100% phone number capture, maintain a lightweight 2-step companion Zap:

1. **Trigger:** `Yelp Leads` ➔ **`Phone Availability`**
   * Fires specifically when Yelp exposes the customer's direct phone number.
2. **Action:** `LeadConnector` ➔ **`Add/Update Contact`**
   * **Email:** `Temporary Email` (`leadsapi+...`) from Step 1 *(used to match the existing GHL contact)*.
   * **Phone Number:** `Phone Number` from Step 1.
   * **Mark as Lead:** `Yes` (True).

## 4. Custom Field & Survey Questionnaire Mapping

When mapping Yelp survey questions (Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Cleaning Frequency) in Zapier:

### The Dynamic Index Issue
Yelp passes survey questions as an array (`Survey Answers [0]`, `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`):
* Different job types (e.g. "Deep Cleaning" vs "Standard Home Cleaning") present different questions in varying orders.
* Mapping fixed array positions (e.g. assuming `[2]` is always Bedrooms) can cause values to shift or appear blank if question orders change.

### Best Practices:
1. Ensure the Zap is published on the latest version (not left in Draft).
2. For mission-critical fields, map `Project Job Names` directly to `Yelp Service Type`.
3. Map `Project Summary` to `Yelp Notes / Customer Request` as a complete fallback reference.

## 5. GoHighLevel Workflow Guardrails (Stopping `( / )`)

When auto-responding to new leads via GHL Workflows:

### The Timing Buffer & Fallback Logic
1. **Add a Wait Step (1 to 2 minutes):**
   * Position a 60–120 second delay before the first automated message action.
   * Allows Yelp's `Phone Availability` trigger and questionnaire data to settle on the contact record before the message sends.
2. **If/Else Message Routing:**
   * **Branch A (Custom fields populated):**
     > *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, thanks for reaching out to Mum's Cleaning Services Chicago on Yelp! We received your request for a {{contact.yelp_service_type}} ({{contact.yelp_bedrooms}} / {{contact.yelp_bathrooms}}). Can you provide the square footage of your home so we can produce an accurate quote?"*
   * **Branch B (Fallback if fields were omitted by user):**
     > *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, thanks for reaching out to Mum's Cleaning Services Chicago on Yelp! Could you share how many bedrooms/bathrooms you have and your approximate square footage so we can prepare an accurate quote?"*

## 6. Verification Checklist for New Deployments

- [ ] Zap 1 (`Yelp Leads to Go High Level Contact`) is **Published** (Status: On).
- [ ] Companion Zap (`Phone Availability ➔ LeadConnector Update`) is **Published** (Status: On).
- [ ] Redundant Email AI Zaps creating `reply+...` contacts are **Disabled**.
- [ ] GHL Workflow greeting contains a 1-minute buffer and conditional fallback branch.
- [ ] Outbound replies from GHL Conversations verify receipt in the consumer Yelp app chat.
